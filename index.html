03 / Calculation method

Weight-based doses are multiplied by the entered weight. Minute-based doses are converted to an hourly amount. mg is converted to µg when needed, then the hourly drug amount is divided by concentration to produce mL/h.

Weight-based: dose × weight, with unit/time conversion, ÷ concentration. Absolute dose: dose per hour, with unit/time conversion, ÷ concentration.
